About Ganchior World

Established in 2008 under the G.P.R. Educational Trust, Ganchior Matriculation Higher Secondary School stands as a beacon of academic excellence in Thiruvallur. We are committed to delivering holistic, globally benchmarked education that nurtures intellect, character, and innovation.

Our distinctive pedagogy blends the US Curriculum (LKG – Grade V) with Samacheer Kalvi (Grade VI – XII), delivered in an English-medium environment. With integrated NEET coaching, Robotics & Coding programs, and a focus on 21st-century skills, we empower students to become confident, future-ready leaders.
